## Event-based intelligence

WHO Disease Outbreak News API:

https://www.who.int/api/default/diseaseoutbreaknews

WHO API documentation:
https://www.who.int/api/hubs/diseaseoutbreaknews/sfhelp

## Climate

NASA POWER daily API:

https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/

The prototype retrieves monthly climate context for representative country points. These are not gridded national averages.

## Population

World Bank Indicators API:

https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/all/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL

No API key is required.

## Important licensing / attribution

Check the original provider terms before redistributing raw data. WHO datasets may carry specific attribution and use conditions. This repository should cite the original providers in any public deployment.
